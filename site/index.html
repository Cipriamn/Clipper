Clipper

A private, single-user tool. Not offered to the public.

Clipper is a personal video-editing pipeline run by its owner on their own computer. It helps the owner cut short clips from long-form videos they have the rights to use, and, after the owner manually approves each clip, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The app has exactly one user: its owner. It does not access, collect or manage any other person's account or data.
